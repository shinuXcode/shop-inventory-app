CORE WORKFLOWS

Built around the work a shop actually does.

₹

Fast billing

Search by name, SKU or barcode, edit quantities, apply discounts, choose payment method and checkout without leaving the flow.

▣

Inventory

Manage SKUs, pricing, taxes, stock, categories and low-stock thresholds with responsive desktop and mobile layouts.

◎

Customers

Store customer details, edit records and view purchase history directly from the app.

▤

Invoices

Open invoice details and print, share or save A4 documents and compact thermal receipts.

↗

Analytics

See today’s sales, invoice count, average bill, low-stock items and a seven-day sales view.

⌁

Keyboard ready

Desktop-friendly shortcuts keep the billing counter fast: F2 search, F4 customer, F8 hold, F9 payment and F12 checkout.

LOCAL-FIRST

Your counter keeps working when the network doesn't.

Business data is written to local SQLite first. Cloud sync is optional, authenticated and retryable, so internet loss does not block everyday billing.
