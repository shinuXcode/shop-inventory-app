HOW IT WORKS

A simple four-step counter flow.

01Find product

Search name, SKU or barcode.

02Build cart

Adjust quantity and customer details.

03Take payment

Cash, UPI, card or credit.

04Issue invoice

Print, save or share the invoice.

LOCAL-FIRST + CLOUD SYNC

Work offline. Sync when connected.

Your business data is stored locally first. With an account and workspace, SBILL automatically synchronizes changes between supported devices through Supabase.

DEVICE → SQLITE → SYNC QUEUE → CLOUD → DEVICES
Account basedBusiness-scoped RLSAutomatic retryConflict aware

Use the same SBILL account on every supported device to restore the business workspace and synchronize it.
